Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about booking, payments, technician verification, and cancellation policies.

Every service professional on HomeBuddy must undergo multi-step KYC verification. We collect government identity documents (Aadhaar/PAN), trade certifications, and police clearance verification. Documents are manually vetted by platform administrators before awarding the green Verified Professional badge.

HomeBuddy supports both Online Payment (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking via Razorpay test mode) and Cash on Service (COS) where you hand cash directly to the technician after inspecting the completed job.

To eliminate disputes and guarantee quality, our technicians are mandated to photograph the workspace before commencing work and immediately after completion. Customers review these comparison photographs in their dashboard before granting final sign-off.

For critical issues like water leaks, power circuit failure, or locked doors, Emergency Dispatch broadcasts your request immediately to all active verified professionals within your pincode radius, prioritizing immediate response within 45 minutes.

Bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 4 hours prior to the scheduled slot. If online payment was completed, refunds are processed back to the original source.
