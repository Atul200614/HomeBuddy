Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect personal contact details (name, phone, email, delivery addresses) solely for dispatching services and generating invoices. Professional KYC documents are stored in secure, restricted vaults.

2. Private Storage of Work Proofs

Photographs taken inside private customer residences for work proof are strictly accessible only to the customer, the assigned technician, and support staff for dispute investigation.

3. Payment Security

HomeBuddy does not store sensitive credit/debit card numbers or CVVs. All electronic transactions are processed via Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant secure vault.
